01

Information We Collect

When you create an account or use TutorVerse, we may collect information that helps us provide and improve our services.

This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Account and profile information
  • Learning preferences and interests
  • Tutor qualifications and professional information
  • Information you voluntarily provide through the platform
02

How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through TutorVerse to operate, maintain, and improve the platform.

  • Connect students with suitable tutors
  • Help tutors provide educational services
  • Manage user accounts and profiles
  • Support course and booking functionality
  • Communicate important updates and service-related information
  • Improve the functionality and experience of TutorVerse
03

Tutor Information

Tutors may provide professional information to help students make informed decisions when choosing a tutor.

This may include qualifications, subjects, teaching experience, location, profile pictures, availability, and other professional information.

Some tutor profile information may be publicly visible to other users of TutorVerse.

04

Information Protection

TutorVerse takes reasonable measures to help protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account, including keeping login credentials private.

05

Third-Party Services

TutorVerse may use trusted third-party services to support certain features of the platform, including authentication, hosting, communication, analytics, and other technical services.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

06

Your Choices

You may review and update certain information associated with your TutorVerse account.

If you have questions about your information, wish to update your details, or have concerns about how your information is handled, you can contact the TutorVerse team.

TutorVerse may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our platform, services, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page together with the updated date.
